Use Offline

If you'd like to use this app without an internet connection, open it in your browser and download or save this webpage to your device (most browsers: menu → "Save page" or "Download page") while you still have Wi-Fi or a data connection — ideally shortly before you travel.

Keep in mind: without an internet connection, the exchange rate can't refresh. Download it while you're still online so you start your trip with a reasonably current rate — it won't update again until you're back online.
